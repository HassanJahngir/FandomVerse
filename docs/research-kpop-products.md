# K-Pop and merchandise research

Verified against the linked source pages on 2026-09-26. The dataset is an editorial snapshot, not a live feed. Historical events and releases are labelled. K-Pop profiles use real artists (BTS members), interpreting the SRS's character-profile requirement appropriately for this category. No personality claims, rumours or invented quotations are used.

The five profiles draw on BIGHIT MUSIC's group profile and individual official album pages. The article is original listening-guide commentary with factual anchors to those pages. Three distinct historical events are included: the Busan concert, the Proof exhibition and the 2023 anniversary FESTA. Each event has a date, location, source and past status. The trailer links to the rights holder's page because independent embedding permission has not yet been established. The 2022 release is not presented as upcoming.

Merchandise prices are USD snapshots from official retailer or publisher pages. A single named variant is recorded for each item; no discounts, taxes, shipping or stock availability are asserted. Prices may change after verification. The shirt uses the displayed common listed price for size M. VIZ uses the explicitly priced digital edition, not its paperback list price. FandomVerse is not an official retailer. Product photographs are omitted because public access did not establish permission to republish them. The renderer must clearly show the source-link alternative.

## Asset acquisition status

Two public-domain White House BTS photographs and four Commons-licensed artist portraits were subsequently downloaded, resized, and converted to WebP and AVIF. Their individual source pages, creators, licenses, dates, and changes are recorded in `assets-remaining.json` and `assets-kpop-portraits.json`. Jimin's profile uses a clearly described BTS group photograph because a reusable solo portrait was not established.

## Important limitations

- Recheck prices and dated announcements before the competition.
- K-Pop coverage begins with BTS, not a claim to represent every artist or group.
- Photographs and playable local media are tracked separately from factual research.
- No merchandise photo is currently bundled; permission is an explicit remaining gap.
- No unsourced future release dates were added to fill a calendar.
