## Permission gaps

Official standalone character artwork, franchise key art, and the five exact merchandise product photographs could not be independently cleared for bundling. Profiles use correctly labelled fan cosplay or performer photos where licensed. Some event and release cards use clearly labelled subject-related context visuals instead of pretending the image depicts that event or product. All 104 card images are now local, and every product card displays labelled franchise context imagery; exact product photographs remain at official retailer links. Official trailer players and links still load online when opened, while their cards show local, licensed fan or venue photos rather than inaccessible YouTube thumbnails. An official page's public visibility is not permission to republish its images. The site does not assert exclusive, official, or retailer status. Only the reusable Tales of Zestiria trailer provides local audio/video; other categories use official online players where authorized. The team should request rights-holder permission before replacing the context images with exact product photographs.
