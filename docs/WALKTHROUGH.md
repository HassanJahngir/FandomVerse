# Judge walkthrough and MP4 shot list

The recorded `submission/FandomVerse-demo.mp4` shows a representative working browser flow. For a live presentation, start with `npm run preview` after `npm run build` and use these talking points.

1. **Home and seven worlds:** Introduce “Seven Worlds. One FandomVerse.” Show the seven portal links, featured article, events, trailer, live clock, simulated visitor counter, and pause-motion control.
2. **World hubs:** Open Anime, Gaming, Movies, TV Shows, K-Pop, Comics, and Manga. Explain the local JSON records, category accent colors, cards, type/tag filters, and alphabetical/newest/featured sort. Note that profiles and events meet the seven-world count.
3. **Global search:** Search “Tanjiro”, combine Anime and Profile, open the result, then try an unmatched query to show the empty state. Search remains reachable from the header.
4. **Profiles and articles:** On a sourced profile, show series, bio, traits, related items, and source links. Open a long-form article and explain that its wording is original commentary, with citations.
5. **Gallery and media:** Open each world's gallery; demonstrate arrow keys, Escape, credits, and focus return. Show the locally playable licensed Tales of Zestiria trailer, poster, audio excerpt, native controls, and transcript/description. Explain that other media opens an official page or authorized embed when reuse rights were unavailable.
6. **Events and releases:** Show past status and source/location details. Open the release calendar and step to a confirmed upcoming official date. Never describe historical entries as current news.
7. **Trailers:** Use category and release-status filters; open a player on demand and show the official source link and unavailable-media fallback.
8. **Merchandise/cart:** Add a real named variant, change quantity, show subtotal/total in USD, remove it, and state that purchase happens only through the official retailer link; FandomVerse has no checkout.
9. **Bookmarks and notes:** Save a profile, open Saved, add a note, export the formatted list, remove a bookmark. Explain bookmarks use `localStorage` while notes use `sessionStorage` and disappear in a new browser session.
10. **Orbit:** Ask a suggested or typed factual question, follow a result link, and try an unknown question. Explain that JSON FAQs and recommendation rules drive a scripted assistant, without an AI service.
11. **About/Contact:** Show project and AI acknowledgement, the participant-supplied Warriors Xtreme details, and the DHA Karachi Google Map. Trigger geolocation and demonstrate a denied/unavailable message without claiming the location was captured.
12. **Demo account and access:** Open login/signup and point out that no real account is created, no password is requested/stored, and all routes remain available. Show keyboard focus, phone layout, and reduced-motion settings if judges ask.

The MP4 is a demonstration of an actual browser session, captured from the production preview. It cannot replace a final participant check of time-sensitive prices and release dates or the team configuration.
